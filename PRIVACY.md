# Privacy Policy — Hermes Notes Sync

Last updated: 2026-11-27

**Hermes Notes Sync** is a personal, single-user application used by its owner to sync personal study notes between Google Drive and local tools.

- The application requests only Google Drive access, used solely to read and write the owner's own documents.
- It does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal data.
- It does not use any third-party analytics or advertising services.
- OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's local machine.
